## What to fix next
_(Coach updates this each session — the ONE thing to work on next time.)_
- **#1: End your sentences.** One thought, full stop, next thought. Long fused sentences
  are the main thing costing clarity. Trick that works: give the answer a **structure**
  ("it depends → case A → case B") — structure shortens sentences automatically.
- When a word comes out wrong or won't come: **pause and restart the phrase clean**
  ("…a better place *than* Amazon, I mean"). Don't push through or trail off.
- Watch simple past tense for weekend/casual talk ("I made," not "I have made").
