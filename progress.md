## What to fix next
_(Coach updates this each session — the ONE thing to work on next time.)_
- **#1: One idea per sentence.** When the point pivots to something new ("weekdays are
  different, though"), end the sentence and start fresh — don't bend one sentence around the
  turn. Rambling only shows up when 2–3 ideas crowd into one sentence; single-idea sentences
  came out crisp on their own ("I don't go out Sunday night").
- **When the list or thought is done, STOP.** Trailing off happens because stopping feels
  abrupt, so a closing phrase ("And that's really it.") gives permission to stop cleanly.
- Structure is still the master lever: "it depends → case A → case B" shortens sentences
  automatically.
- When a word comes out wrong or won't come: **pause and restart the phrase clean.**
