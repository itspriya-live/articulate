# Articulate 🎙️ — Personal Speaking & Articulation Training

Daily practice to speak clearly and explain ideas clearly — in conversations and public speaking.
Sibling project to [Wordsy](https://github.com/itspriya-live/wordsy) (vocabulary).

## What's here
| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `ARTICULATE-LLM-GUIDE.md` | Paste into any LLM to make it coach you exactly as before |
| `progress.md` | Live tracker: sessions, streak, filler-word trend, what to fix next |
| `phrasebook.md` | Collected ready-to-use phrases — different ways to express a thought |

## How it works
- **Daily (~5 min):** a back-and-forth conversation rep with the LLM role-playing the other
  person, then sharp feedback on rambling, hedging, and structure.
- **Anytime:** ask for a quick phrase you can drop into real conversation or a talk; it gets
  saved to `phrasebook.md`.
- **Delivery/voice:** record yourself on your phone, paste the transcript or a self-score
  (filler count, pace), and the coach logs the trend.

## Resume on a new machine / new LLM
1. Clone this repo.
2. Tell your LLM: *"Read `ARTICULATE-LLM-GUIDE.md` and the data files, confirm where I left
   off from `progress.md`, then let's do a 5-minute conversation rep."*
3. After each session, have it update `progress.md` and commit.
